02 / Teaching approach

Understand the why.
Then solve the how.

A useful session goes beyond the answer to a single problem. The focus is on the concepts, decisions, and reasoning you can use on the next one.

Successful tutoring should eventually make the tutor unnecessary. The goal is understanding and independent problem-solving, with progressively less assistance.

  1. Identify the conceptual gap.

    Start with your questions. If you cannot identify what is confusing, work backward to what you do understand and find where the gap begins.

  2. Rebuild the underlying concept.

    Repair missing prerequisites before moving forward. Adapt the explanation, examples, and pace to the student.

  3. Work through representative problems.

    Connect theory to practice, then have the student work through problems with decreasing assistance.

  4. Explain the reasoning yourself.

    Talk through the solution and test your understanding with guiding questions.

  5. Develop an independent strategy.

    Identify patterns, practice a related problem, and decide what to work on next.
