Ask about tutoring

For course questions, recurring sessions, or in-person availability, include the subject, course level, goals, and preferred times. For in-person tutoring, include your city or ZIP code so we can confirm coverage before booking.

Contact support about tutoring

Prepare for your session

Email relevant problem sets, class notes, topics, and attempted solutions before your session when possible. Include the session date and any upcoming exam topics.

Payment or meeting access

Check your Calendly confirmation for booking and online meeting details. If you cannot find the meeting link or have a payment question, email support with the session date and a brief description of the issue.

Get in touch

Email support.

For questions before booking or help with an existing session, contact us at:

support@elitelambdatutoring.com

What to include

  • Your name and the email address used for booking, if applicable.
  • The session date, time, and time zone for an existing booking.
  • A brief description of your question or the issue you encountered.
  • For a tutoring inquiry, the subject and course level.

Please do not email passwords or full payment card details.

Occasional short academic questions are welcome. Substantive instruction should generally wait until your tutoring session.
