Getting started

Primarily high-school students (including standard, honors, and AP courses) and college students. Mathematics, physics, engineering, and other majors taking quantitative courses are welcome. Graduate students are considered case-by-case.

Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Precalculus, Calculus, Statistics, and Physics. Calculus coverage extends through Calculus III / Multivariable Calculus and Differential Equations. Advanced college mathematics requests can be discussed individually.

You do not need to already be good at math to work with me. Students who are struggling, failing, or missing prerequisites are welcome. We can work backward from what you understand to find and repair the foundational gap. Sometimes a different explanation or pace is what is needed.

Online tutoring is available nationwide using screen sharing and a digital whiteboard. In-person tutoring is offered at the student’s home within selected DMV (Washington, D.C., Maryland, and Virginia) service areas. Public meeting locations are also available by agreement. Contact support to confirm service-area availability and agree on the meeting location before booking. Online meeting details are provided through Calendly.

Preparing for your session

Whenever possible, email homework or problem sets, class notes, current topics, and the material causing difficulty. Include your attempted solutions and any upcoming exam topics so we can focus the session.

Yes. We can clarify concepts, identify errors, work through analogous problems, and prepare with permitted practice materials. You remain responsible for your own work. I do not complete graded assignments, provide answers to active graded examinations, or take quizzes or tests for students.

Notes or practice may be provided when useful, but are not guaranteed after every session. Occasional short questions by email are welcome; substantive instruction should generally wait until the tutoring session.

Booking and payment

Sessions are 60 minutes. Contact the tutor for current rates. The same rate applies online and in person, with a minimum booking of one session. There are no packages, recurring-session discounts, group rates, or separate exam-preparation premiums at launch.

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and no-shows incur a charge of 50% of the session price. If I must cancel, you will not be charged; we can reschedule, and if a reasonable replacement time cannot be arranged, a full refund or credit will be provided.

I will generally provide the full 60 minutes when circumstances allow. The session may need to end at the originally scheduled time if another student is booked immediately afterward or an agreed public meeting location is closing.

Payment is due at booking and is collected through Calendly. Follow the payment steps in the booking calendar to complete your reservation.

Yes, recurring sessions are available. Email support to discuss a regular time. Availability, in-home service-area coverage, and any agreed public meeting location must be confirmed.
